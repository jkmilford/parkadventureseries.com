Yellowstone Bison Trek

Lead the migration: graze, swim, and stand your ground across the world's first national park.

The Great Migration

Winter is coming to the high country. You are a 2,000-pound bison bull in Lamar Valley, and your herd has already moved toward the warm geyser basins in the southwest. Cross the whole park to reach them — the way your kind has for thousands of years.

  • Desktop: W A S D / ←↑↓→ to move, hold Shift to charge.
  • Phone & tablet: touch and drag anywhere to steer, hold the Charge button to run. Tap ⛶ to go fullscreen.
  • Graze: stand on the glowing golden grass tufts to refill energy — walking sips it, charging gulps it.
  • Swim the Yellowstone River, cross the road at the bison jam, and skirt the thermal pools.
  • Wolves shadow the route — and they are deadly: four bites will bring you down. They won't follow you into the river. Stop and face one to stand your ground, charge it, or double-tap the screen to shake it off. Wolves stalk for a moment before they charge — use those seconds.
  • Watch the wolf strike meter — four strikes and it's over. But the herd tends its own: press the ❤ button (or H) and stand still while a herdmate licks a wound clean. And a single spare heart is hidden somewhere in the park — find it, and it will absorb what would be a killing strike. A hidden herd token banks you a bonus stampede charge, too.
  • You start with one herdmate, and another bison joins your band at every flag. Turn and face a predator and the whole band squares up with you to drive it off — a herd defends as a wall, not with its back turned.
  • The trail winds through Norris, Fountain Paint Pot, Midway, and Old Faithful — and a grizzly works the last basins. Nothing bluffs a bear: three bison facing it shoulder to shoulder will make it back off — but it takes two such stands before it truly gives up. A full Herd Call stampede settles it in one shot. Otherwise, run.
  • Tap 🗺️ any time to pause and see exactly where you are on a true map of the park.
  • Herd Call (the bison button, or E): once per trek, summon the herd to stampede through and scatter every predator. Old bulls whisper that one more call waits far to the southeast, off every trail…
