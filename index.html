The Author

Hi, I'm Jeremy

I created the National Park Adventure Series because I believe national park trips are some of the most powerful experiences a family can share — and I wanted to build books that make those moments even richer.

Each book is packed with carefully researched trivia, puzzles, and activities designed to spark curiosity and conversation — whether you're on the trail, at the campsite, or in the backseat on the way there.

The Great Smoky Mountains book is where it started. With Yellowstone, Grand Canyon, Yosemite, and Rocky Mountain coming soon, the whole collection covers America's most iconic parks.
